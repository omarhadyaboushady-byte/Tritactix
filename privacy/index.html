How Tritactix collects, uses and protects the information you share with us through tritactix.tech.

Updated October 20262 min read
01

What we collect

  • What you send us through the contact form: your name, phone number, company and message.
  • Messages you send us by email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • Anonymous visit statistics: pages viewed, referring site, country and device type. These are collected without cookies and cannot identify you.
02

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To deliver the services or products you ask for.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it.

We never sell your personal information.

03

Who processes it for us

Web3Forms delivers contact-form messages to our inbox. Vercel hosts this website and provides our visit statistics. Both process data on our behalf and may store it outside Egypt or the UAE.

04

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need to respond and work with you, and no longer than three years after our last contact, unless the law requires longer.

05

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email consultant@tritactix.tech and we will reply within 30 days.
